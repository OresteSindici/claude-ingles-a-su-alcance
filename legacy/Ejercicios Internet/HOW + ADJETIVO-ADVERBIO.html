"How" + Adjetivo/adverbio

Utilice las cajas de la izquierda para digitar el literal de la respuesta escogida entre las opciones de la derecha.
Luego haga "click" en el botón "Verificar respuestas".

1. ___ letters has the secretary typed?
2. ___ is it to the nearest drugstore?
3. ___ do they owe you?
4. ___ going out tonight?
5. ___ is your grandma? She looks so young!
6. ___ does it take you to get to school by bike?
7. ___ is the Magdalena River? Can we reach its bottom?
8. ___ do the meetings take place? Every week?

 a. How much
  b. How long
  c. How often
 d. How many
 e. How about
 f.  How deep
 g. How far
 h. How old

La expresión "How about" significa "Qué te parece"

o "Qué opinas?
